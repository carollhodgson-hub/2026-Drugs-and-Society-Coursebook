Using APA Style

Learn when to cite, how to paraphrase responsibly, and how to connect an in-text citation to a complete reference.

Why sociology uses APA

APA style helps readers distinguish your analysis from information drawn from another source. Cite facts, statistics, theories, research findings, cases, definitions, and paraphrased ideas. A citation does not replace your explanation: introduce the evidence, explain it in your own words, and show how it supports your sociological point.

Direct quotes should be rare in sociology assignments. Use your own words to demonstrate understanding. If the exact wording is essential, use quotation marks and include a page number or another locator.

Citing the new coursebooks

Because these are instructor-authored online coursebooks, cite the authors, year, italicized title, and direct URL. Use a hanging indent on the References page.

References

Hodgson, C. B. (2026). Beyond the substance: Understanding drugs in culture & society. https://soc245-what-is-a-drug.carollhodgson.chatgpt.site/

3. Paraphrase and apply

Imagine the source explains that family stress is shaped by both demands and available resources. Before revealing the model, say the idea in genuinely new language and connect it to a family example. Do not merely replace a few words.

Model: A family’s response to a crisis depends not only on the event but also on its interpretation and access to support (Hodgson & Norwood, 2026). For example, reliable childcare and paid leave may help caregivers adapt to a medical emergency without losing employment.

Notice that the model includes the source idea, a citation, and the student’s own application.

Build the References page

  • Center and bold the heading References.
  • Alphabetize entries by the first author’s surname or organization name.
  • Double-space the page and use a hanging indent for each entry.
  • Include every source cited in the assignment—and cite every source listed.
  • Use sentence case for titles of books, webpages, and reports.
  • Check generated citations carefully; citation tools and AI can make mistakes.

Quick final check

  • Did I write the explanation and application in my own words?
  • Did I cite every borrowed fact, idea, theory, statistic, image, and case?
  • Does every in-text citation connect to a complete reference?
  • Did I use quotations only when the exact wording was necessary?
  • Did I protect the privacy of people discussed in my work?
